# The Mathematics behind Modern AI

A short, accessible presentation on the ideas that power modern artificial intelligence.

## Overview

This presentation demystifies the mathematics behind AI by explaining how models are built, trained, and improved. Rather than treating AI as a mysterious black box, it walks through the core concepts behind neural networks, learning rules, and modern architectures.

The talk covers:

- the idea of AI as a parameterized function
- multilayer perceptrons and activation functions
- gradient descent and backpropagation
- convolutional neural networks and image processing
- transformers and attention
- alternative paradigms such as KANs, spiking neural networks, evolutionary algorithms, Bayesian models, and neuro-symbolic AI
